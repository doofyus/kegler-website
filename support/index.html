Common questions

How do I enter a ball?

Tap how many pins fell, or mark the pins that fell on the deck. Marking the pins adds your leaves and pocket to Stats. To fix a ball, tap its frame on the score sheet.

How many nights are free?

Your first five nights are free; each day you bowl counts as one. After that, one purchase unlocks Kegler for good. Your games and stats stay either way.

I reinstalled Kegler or got a new iPhone.

Open Settings → Restore purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought Kegler with. Your games come back from iCloud on their own.

Can I bring games from another app?

Yes: Settings → Import games takes a PinPal backup, a LeagueSecretary export or a spreadsheet.

How do I get my games out?

Settings → Export games as CSV gives you every game in a file you can keep or open anywhere.

How is my handicap worked out?

From your league's basis and percentage, set when you add the league, and your league average before tonight. The summary shows the handicap a game and the average it came from.
