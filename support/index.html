Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to bowling@ratsep.org. Every message is read and answered, usually within a few days.

In the app, Settings → Send feedback writes the email for you, with your app and iOS version.

Common questions

How do I enter a ball?

Tap how many pins fell, or tap the pins that fell on the deck. Marking the pins adds your leaves and pocket to Stats.

How many nights are free?

Your first five nights are free, each day you bowl counts as one. After that, one purchase unlocks Kegler for good. Your games and stats stay either way.

I bought Kegler on another device or reinstalled it.

Go to Settings → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can I bring games from another app?

Yes: Settings → Import games takes a PinPal backup, a LeagueSecretary export or a spreadsheet.

How do I get my games out?

Settings → Export games as CSV gives you every game in a file you can keep or open anywhere.
