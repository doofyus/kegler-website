Kegler

Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

In short: Kegler collects nothing about you. Your games live on your devices and in your own iCloud. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer never sees your data.

Who we are

Kegler is made by Mikk Rätsep, an independent developer in Estonia. You can reach me at bowling@ratsep.org.

Your games and stats

Everything you enter (games, nights, leagues, centers, balls and notes) is stored on your iPhone. When you're signed in to iCloud, Kegler keeps a copy in your private iCloud database so your games sync across your devices. That database belongs to your Apple Account: Apple stores it, and neither the developer nor anyone else can read it. Kegler has no server of its own.

Location

If you allow it, Kegler uses your location while the app is open to find bowling centers near you and to pick the one you're at when you start a session. Searches go to Apple Maps. Your location is not saved; only the centers you add, with their place, are kept with your games. You can turn location off at any time in iOS Settings, and Kegler works without it.

Photos

When you save a night's picture, Kegler asks to add it to your Photos. It can only add pictures; it can't see your library.

Purchases

The full version is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; Kegler only learns whether the purchase went through, never your payment details.

Feedback email

If you write to us, from the app or otherwise, we receive your email address and what you send, including the app and iOS version the app adds to the message. It's used only to answer you and to improve Kegler, and is never shared or sold.

Importing and exporting

Files you import, and the CSV files you export, stay on your device or wherever you choose to put them. Nothing is uploaded.

Third parties

Kegler includes no third-party code that collects data: no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The only outside services it uses are Apple's (iCloud, Apple Maps and the App Store), under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept for as long as you keep it. You can delete any game or night in the app. Deleting Kegler removes its data from your iPhone; to remove the iCloud copy too, go to iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Kegler. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete, but you're welcome to ask at bowling@ratsep.org.

Children

Kegler isn't directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
