Kegler

Your bowling, kept. Every game, every league night, and the numbers that show where your pins go.

Coming soon to the App Store

Built for bowling night

Quick between turns

Tap how many fell, or mark the pins that fell on the deck. The frames and running total fill in as you go, and any ball is fixed by tapping its frame.

League nights

Your series, handicap and league average, the lanes you were on, and how tonight compares with last week. Tournaments too, with where you finished.

Stats that point somewhere

Not just percentages: which spares cost you, how much a better 10 pin is worth a game, and, when you mark the pins, your leaves and how often you find the pocket.

Nights worth remembering

A summary after every night, your high games and series called out, and a picture of your series to share with the league.

Your arsenal

Note which ball you threw and see how each one strikes, on league nights and in practice.

On your Lock Screen

During a league night, your game and series stay on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Bring your games

Import from PinPal, LeagueSecretary or a spreadsheet, and export every game as CSV whenever you like.

On all your devices

Your games sync through your own iCloud, with no account to make.

Price

5 nights free

Then one purchase unlocks Kegler for good. No subscription, Family Sharing included, and your games and stats stay yours either way.
