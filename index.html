A ball in one tap.

Tap how many fell between turns, or mark the pins that fell on the deck. The frames and running total fill in as you go, and any ball is fixed by tapping its frame.

1 2 3 4 5 6 8 9 Leaves 7-10 Split 8 pins down

Every night, summed up.

Your series and how it compares with last week, your league average moving, and the handicap a game with the average it came from. High games are called out, and a picture of the night is ready to share with the league.

Your leagues, week by week.

Each league keeps its nights, lanes, average and next handicap, from week one to the end of the season. Tournaments too, with where you finished.

Stats that say what to work on.

Not just percentages: which spares cost you, how much a better 10 pin is worth a game, how your first game compares with the rest. Mark the pins, and your leaves and pocket join in.

Also in Kegler

Your arsenal

Note which ball you threw and see how each one strikes, in league and in practice.

On your Lock Screen

During a league night, your game and series stay on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Bring your games

Import from PinPal, LeagueSecretary or a spreadsheet, and export every game as CSV.

On all your devices

Your games sync through your own iCloud, with no account to make.

Any slice of your bowling

Look at one league, one center, one pair of lanes or one ball at a time.

Knows your center

Picks the center you're at when you start, and remembers your notes from last time there.

5 nights free

Then one purchase unlocks Kegler for good. No subscription, Family Sharing included, and your games and stats stay yours either way.

Your games stay yours.

No account, no ads and no tracking. Your games live on your devices and in your own iCloud, and the developer never sees them.
